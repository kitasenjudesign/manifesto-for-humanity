## Leave room for human hands

* Leave room for humans to step in.
* Don't have AI make the output itself; use AI in the process of making.
* Build tools of your own.
* Feed your own data to AI and have it regenerate it.
* Even AI-generated images and videos: rework them afterward. Treat them as material.
* Tidy code so humans can read it. Try reading the code AI wrote.
* Have AI mass-produce, then choose from what it makes.

## Do what AI is bad at

* Think about what you're obsessively fond of, not the average or the model student.
* Think locally: your surroundings, within a five-meter radius.
* Make something with your hands and body.
* Rebel against clichés and conventions.

## Enjoy uncertainty

* Actively make use of AI's errors and hallucinations.
* Deliberately enter abstract prompts and use the results as chance.
* Let AI pull you outside your own imagination. Bouncing ideas off a bumpy wall.
* AI as a chance operation.

## Value smallness

* Make things so "small" that even typing a prompt feels like too much trouble.
* Make works that take no more effort than writing a prompt.
* Learn from the smallness of haiku and tanka.

## Compare

* Compare what you made by hand with what AI made.
* Try making the same thing. Observe the difference.

## Ask to be taught

* Don't leave what you made with AI as a black box; have AI teach you how it works.
* Try extending, modifying, and applying those principles.

## Let AI handle the tedious parts

* Hand over to AI the parts you used to find tedious, the parts that were bottlenecks.
* Use the time freed up to rest or to work on something else.
* Work on the parts whose quality you could never raise before.

## Use it like crazy

* Accept AI, and for now, just use it like crazy.
* Then write down how it felt.
* Record and observe this period of change brought about by AI.

## Cultivate an aesthetic eye

* Try choosing from the huge volume of things AI has made. See choosing as part of making.
* An aesthetic eye is a hierarchy. Look at lots of things and rank them in your own way.
* Agonize while you make. Agonize, then reach a conclusion.

## Dig into AI as a medium

* Think about what can only be done with AI.
* Push hallucinations and AI-ness even further to create an excessive AI style.
* Think about the aesthetics of generative AI.

## Look at inefficiency

* Turn your attention to things other than making money, saving time, and getting value for money or for time.
* Aim for "goodness," not "time-saving."

## Struggle

* Be aware of the painful parts of making, the parts where you feel things aren't going well.
* Be conscious of getting past them.
